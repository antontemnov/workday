rail
A persistent vertical rail à la VS Code/Cursor. Strong IDE rhyme and scales to more views, but costs ~36px of horizontal space — meaningful at 640px wide where every column matters. Best if more than 3 views are ever planned.
C · alternative bottom dock · tab bar
A 36px dock at the bottom with icon + label. Most discoverable thanks to text labels, and very mobile-app familiar. Trades vertical space (shorter session list) and pulls focus away from the timeline. Less native for desktop tray apps.
recommendation

Mirror the weekday-grid

The header is already a 3-column grid (1fr · auto · 1fr) with column 3 empty. Drop a small bordered container there that visually echoes the weekday-grid on the left. Result: the centered date stays anchored between two symmetrical caps. Click the icons below to walk through the three destination screens.

Why this works

  • Zero new visual vocabulary — same pill, same cells, same active style.
  • Slots into the grid's empty column 3 — no structural change.
  • Centered date stays centered; visual symmetry improves.
  • Persistent — visible from every view, no open/close interaction.
  • Compact: ~70px on the right, leaves the timeline and cards untouched.

Trade-offs

  • Icons-only — discoverability rests on tooltips (acceptable for 3 items).
  • Caps the view count at 3–4 (more would need a different shell).
  • Right cell will compete visually with weekday-grid on very narrow widths.
